Turn off these 9 iPhone settings right now

UPDATED OCT 2026iOS 266 MIN READ
TL;DR

Turn off background app refresh, significant locations and personalized ads for better battery life and privacy. All are in Settings and take about 3 minutes total.

1. Background App Refresh

Apps keep refreshing even when you're not using them. Turn it off for everything except messaging and maps.

Settings → General → Background App Refresh → Off

Slepr tip: Low Power Mode does most of this automatically. Turn it on from Control Center when you're under 30%.

2. Significant Locations

Your iPhone quietly logs places you visit often. Unless you use location-based suggestions, turn it off.

Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → System Services → Significant Locations

3. Personalized Ads

Apple uses your activity to target ads in the App Store and News. Switching it off changes nothing you'll miss.

Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Personalized Ads
